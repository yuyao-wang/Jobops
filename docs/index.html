Apply faster without turning your job search into spam.

Jobops combines Codex orchestration with deterministic Playwright adapters. It keeps routine applications fast and low-token while preserving tailored materials, explicit controls, and verifiable outcomes.

Get started Read the architecture

Private by design

Candidate facts, resumes, browser state, and evidence stay in a local Private Home. ATS/mailbox passwords and the permit secret use macOS Keychain; optional AI-provider tokens are runtime-only.

Deterministic first

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Jobvite, and Workday use structured adapters. Normal recognized forms require no model call, reducing JavaScript-page context and token use.

Quality by tier

Application policy scales effort with job priority: reusable verified materials for routine roles, tailored resumes and narrative cover letters for important roles.

Execution model

Codex decides. Adapters execute. Evidence closes the loop.

01 · QueuePrioritized CSV jobs become leased, resumable runs.
02 · PolicyTier rules select materials and the allowed autonomy level.
03 · FillATS adapters resolve only confirmed facts and verified answers.
04 · ReviewAfter Gate A preflight, read-back validation establishes a persisted Review checkpoint.
05 · SubmitGate B authorizes one click; explicit evidence is required for verified success.
Honest first-release scope

Stable Review, not universal auto-submit.

The first release targets reliable Review arrival on five major ATS platforms. CAPTCHA, account locks, unresolved verification, new sensitive required questions, and confirmation-dependent submissions stop safely. Outreach, cold email, and scheduled follow-up remain optional and disabled.
